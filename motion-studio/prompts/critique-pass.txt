CRITIQUE PASS: Central Square AI motion reel
Run this after every contact sheet. You are now the harshest art director on the account, not the
person who built the film. Judge only what is on the images and in the cue table, not what the code intends.

INPUTS (generate them first, then open every image and actually look):
  node scripts/render.mjs projects/<slug>/film.html --sheet            -> renders/film.beats.png
  node scripts/render.mjs projects/<slug>/film.html --cues             -> cue table + grid warnings
  node scripts/render.mjs projects/<slug>/film.html --strip=A:B        -> one per entrance/transition changed this round
  node scripts/render.mjs projects/<slug>/film.html --at=T --crop-at=<selector>,W,H --zoom=2   -> each piece of small type
  For multi-format reels, also: --sheet --size=1080x1920 and --sheet --size=1080x1080

STEP 1 · Describe before judging (one line per beat frame):
  "<t>s: what the viewer sees · where their eye goes · what is new since the last frame"
  If two consecutive lines have nothing new, and they're more than 4 s apart, that's a variety failure.

STEP 2 · Score 1–10, with one sentence of evidence each (cite frame times):
  1. Hook (first 2 s): one specific, striking image or line by 2.0 s? No empty or logo-only opening.
  2. Readability at phone size: read the sheet at ~260 px per frame. Is every word legible? Does each line
     hold ≥ 0.4 s + 0.3 s per word? Any text under 2.8vmin, crushed by motion, or cut by a mask edge (check crops)?
  3. Motion quality (from strips): anticipation and follow-through? Springs settling, not snapping?
     No linear slides, nothing popping in or out between two frames, never everything moving at once?
  4. Variety: something new on screen every 2–4 s? Does the reel escalate, or repeat one trick?
  5. Brand accuracy: Geist + Geist Mono only, lavender ground, violet the one accent, the headline gradient on at
     most one text line per frame (never on backgrounds or chrome), real logo mark, "EXAMPLE DATA" chips on example figures?
     Real product UI only, captured or supplied, never invented? Every claim sourced in BRIEF.md?
  6. Sound sync: `--cues` shows no off-grid WARNING? Every visible hit is tagged data-sfx, and the ♪ marker
     in strips sits on the frame where the action reads? Nothing loud with no visible cause?

STEP 3 · Banned-defaults sweep (any hit caps Brand accuracy at 5):
  centered title on a gradient · everything fading in · corner labels · frame borders · glow on UI chrome ·
  generic particle bursts · invented UI or data · logo intro before the hook

STEP 4 · Pick the 3 WORST problems (lowest scores first; ties go to what a viewer would notice first).
  For each: frame time(s) · what's wrong · the concrete fix (numbers: seconds, vmin, spring preset, beat index).

STEP 5 · Record the round in SCORECARD.md (pass number, six scores, the three fixes), apply the three fixes,
  then start again at INPUTS.

Stop only when ALL six scores are ≥ 8 AND at least 3 rounds have run. Don't round scores up to finish:
a 7 is a 7. If a problem can't be fixed without Teijas (missing real UI, an unsourced claim, a brief question),
stop and ask instead of inventing.
