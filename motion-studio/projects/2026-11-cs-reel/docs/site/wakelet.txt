IN JAMES'S WORDS

We asked James three questions. These are his answers, word for word.

JAMES AT WAKELET
01
What were you trying to understand about the Wakelet community before we started?

We wanted to understand who was actively engaging with our community, including those who were participating more quietly, rather than making assumptions based on the most visible voices. After changing our subscription model, we needed a clearer picture of usage patterns, sharing and collaboration so we could focus our time and resources on the people who were most engaged with Wakelet.

02
What did Ellie help you do that you couldn't do before, and roughly how much time did it save you?

We had a wealth of information spread across documents, spreadsheets, our own data and HubSpot, making it difficult for the team to build a clear picture of what was happening. Pulling everything together manually, including NPS and CRM reports, could take hours. Ellie helped us automate this process, saving our team at least half a day each week and giving us faster, clearer insights into our community.

“It helps you identify underserved members who might not be the loudest voices in your community.”
JAMES DAVIS · WAKELET
03
What would you say to another community lead thinking about using Ellie?

If your focus is on community and building something that goes beyond one-to-one engagement, Ellie is invaluable. It helps you identify underserved members who might not be the loudest voices in your community, understand their engagement and focus your efforts on the right people. It allows you to move beyond guesswork and make more informed decisions about how to nurture and grow your community.
